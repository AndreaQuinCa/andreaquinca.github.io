Research

Methods for complex, evolving systems

I develop computational methods for inference in high-dimensional state-space models, with interests spanning particle filtering, dependence learning and climate applications.

Selected publications

  1. Conference paper · CAMSAP 2025

    Local Particle Filters: A Unifying Framework and Novel Parallelizable Extensions

    Andrea Quintanilla and Víctor Elvira

    We present a unified framework for local particle filters and discuss new parallelizable extensions.

    DOICAMSAP 2025

  2. Conference paper · EUSIPCO 2025

    State-Space Partitioning via Granger Causality for Local Particle Filters

    Andrea Quintanilla and Víctor Elvira

    A method for learning state-space partitions from Granger-causal structure, supporting local particle filtering without requiring a predefined notion of neighbourhood.

    Paper PDFEUSIPCO proceedings

  3. Preprint · arXiv, 2025

    Local Statistical Parity for the Estimation of Fair Decision Trees

    Andrea Quintanilla and Johan Van Horebeek

    We introduce a node-level fairness criterion for decision trees and show how it relates to global statistical parity. The paper presents the Constrained Logistic Regression Tree (C-LRT) algorithm.

    arXiv:2504.18262

Current directions

Local particle filtering

Designing data-driven partitions that respect the dependency structure of nonlinear state transitions, and studying when a partition should adapt as a system evolves.

Climate tipping points

Developing sequential Bayesian and Monte Carlo approaches for probabilistic forecasts of tipping behaviour, with uncertainty quantification for climate applications.
